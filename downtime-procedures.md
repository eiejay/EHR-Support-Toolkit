# EHR Downtime Procedures

What happens when the EHR is unavailable: planned upgrade or unplanned outage.

## Declaring downtime

- Confirm the outage is system-wide and not one workstation or one unit.
- Notify the house supervisor and unit leadership: start time, scope, expected duration if known.
- Post the downtime status where every unit can see it.

## During downtime: paper workflow

- Switch to the downtime packet: paper MAR, paper order sheets, downtime admission forms.
- Log every medication given, order received, and result phoned in on paper, with time and initials.
- New admissions: complete the paper packet and flag the chart for priority entry at recovery.
- Critical results are phoned and read back, as always.

## Communication rhythm

- Update units at a fixed interval, even if the update is no change.
- One spokesperson for vendor calls; keep the ticket number and vendor case ID in the downtime log.

## Recovery

- Vendor or IT confirms the system is stable before units are told to return.
- Enter downtime documentation in a set order: admissions first, then orders, then MAR entries, then vitals and notes.
- Reconcile: every paper MAR entry must exist in the EHR before the next med pass.
- File the downtime log with the incident record.

## After action

- What failed, what worked, what the paper process missed.
- Update this procedure with anything learned. A downtime that teaches nothing will repeat itself.
