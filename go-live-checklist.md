# Go-Live Support Checklist

For the support analyst on the floor during a go-live or a major upgrade.

## Before the doors open

- Test logins for every role on every unit workstation
- Printers verified: labels, wristbands, MAR and report printers
- Downtime packet stocked on each unit, just in case
- Issue log started: one shared list, numbered, visible to the whole support team
- Escalation path posted: who to call for access, for build, for the vendor

## First hours

- Round every unit on a fixed loop; do not wait in one place for tickets
- Log every issue, even the ones fixed in thirty seconds; patterns only appear in the log
- Separate the three queues: access problems, training questions, true system defects
- Report counts and top issues to leadership at the agreed times

## End of day

- Open issue count by category and owner
- Workarounds in use, written down where the night shift can find them
- Handoff to the next support shift: what is broken, what is being watched, what is promised

## Closing the go-live

- Daily support ends when ticket volume returns to normal levels for two consecutive days
- Final issue log review: every item closed, converted to a ticket, or accepted as a known issue
