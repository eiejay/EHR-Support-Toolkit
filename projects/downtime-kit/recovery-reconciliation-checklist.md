# Recovery Reconciliation Checklist

Purpose: prove that every item documented on paper during downtime now exists in the EHR, in the right chart, with the right times.

## Order of back entry

Work in this order; it puts the highest clinical risk first.

- [ ] New admissions: demographics, orders, allergies, code status
- [ ] Physician and provider orders received during downtime
- [ ] Medication administration: every paper MAR entry, with the actual administration time, not the entry time
- [ ] Critical results and the read-back documentation
- [ ] Vitals, intake and output, and assessments
- [ ] Progress notes
